Why reps work

Discomfort fades. But only through repetition.

Nothing here is a shortcut. These are mechanisms behavioural science has understood for decades — agirep just makes them countable.

01 — HABITUATION

Your body stops reacting

Repeated exposure to the same situation lowers the physiological stress response over time. The first conversation spikes your heart rate. The fiftieth doesn't.

02 — EXPOSURE

Avoidance is the trap

Stepping back from an uncomfortable moment brings instant relief — and makes the next one harder. Facing it repeatedly is the core mechanism behind evidence-based behavioural therapy.

03 — SELF-MONITORING

What gets tracked gets done

Recording a behaviour changes it. Tracking makes small, invisible progress visible — which is exactly the progress most people quit before ever noticing.

agirep is not therapy and makes no clinical claims. It's a tracker — the same way a running app doesn't coach you, it just shows you what you did.
